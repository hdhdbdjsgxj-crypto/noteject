Privacy

This page describes how the Noteject web workspace is designed to handle data. Before cloud accounts and synchronization are enabled for public use, the site owner must configure the listed service providers and publish a reviewed privacy policy that reflects the final deployment and applicable law.

Local workspace

The web app stores its working workspace and imported files in browser storage on the device. Clearing browser data can remove that local copy. Keep a separate backup until cloud synchronization is configured and verified.

AI requests

When enabled, document passages and questions submitted to the AI assistant are sent to Noteject's server and then to OpenAI to generate an answer. API credentials are kept on the server and are not included in the web page. Do not submit documents you are not authorized to share.

Cloud sync

When enabled, account credentials and workspace backups are handled by the configured authentication and storage providers. Sync is optional and requires signing in. This deployment must identify its service providers, retention rules, and contact before accepting real user data.

Contact

Privacy contact details must be added by the site owner before public account registration is enabled.
